Option 1: 1-Click Copy & Paste (No Setup)

Click below to copy this register to your clipboard, then open your Google Sheet, click cell A1, and press Cmd+V or Ctrl+V.

Option 2: Direct Google Sheet Web App Sync (Optional)

Want changes to automatically push into Google Sheets? Add this free 10-line Apps Script in your Google Sheet (Extensions > Apps Script): 

function doPost(e) {
  var d = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  var ss = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet();
  var sh = ss.getSheetByName(d.group) || ss.insertSheet(d.group);
  sh.clear();
  sh.appendRow(d.headers);
  d.rows.forEach(function(r) { sh.appendRow(r); });
  return ContentService.createTextOutput("OK");
}
