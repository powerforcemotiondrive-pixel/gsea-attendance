Google Forms & Sheets Setup

Multi-user concurrent database connection

⚡ Why Google Forms solves multi-user submissions:

When multiple teachers log attendance from their phones simultaneously, direct spreadsheet writing can conflict. Google Forms queues and processes hundreds of simultaneous entries with zero collisions, and automatically streams every record straight into your linked Google Sheet in real time.

Quick Setup (2 Minutes):

  1. Create a new Google Form named "GSEA Attendance" with 7 questions: Promotion, Class Type, Group, Status, Student Name, Date, Notes.
  2. In your Google Form, click the Responses tab → click Link to Sheets (green icon) to create your spreadsheet.
  3. In your linked Google Sheet, click the blue Share button → under General access, change Restricted to "Anyone with the link" (Viewer). This allows the app to retrieve and show all logged absences in real-time.
  4. In the Google Form, click the ⋮ (three dots) at the top right → select "Get pre-filled link". Fill in quick sample text for all 7 items, then click Get link (or Copy link).
  5. Paste that pre-filled link below and click Auto-Connect. That's it!
